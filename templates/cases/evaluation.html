Evaluation Methodology

Information Retrieval performance is measured across standard metrics on top-K retrieved candidate Supreme Court judgments:

  • Precision@5 & Precision@10: Proportion of relevant judgments in top 5 and top 10 results.
  • Recall@K: Ratio of ground-truth relevant judgments successfully retrieved in top-K.
  • Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR): Average of reciprocal ranks of the first relevant judgment.
  • NDCG@10: Normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain accounting for position-weighted relevance.
