Hybrid Retrieval & Reranking Architecture

Legal Lens operates a two-stage hybrid information retrieval architecture designed specifically for legal text mismatch reduction.

Signal 1: Lexical Document Similarity (50%)

Computes cosine similarity between the query TF-IDF vector and all 26,683 judgment vectors in the 120,000-feature space.

CosSim(Q, Doc) = (Q · Doc) / (||Q|| × ||Doc||)

Signal 2: Passage-Level Reranking (35%)

Locates local high-density sentence and paragraph matches within candidate judgment documents.

PassageSim(Q, P_k) = max_k CosSim(Q, P_k)

Signal 3: Legal-Reference Overlap (15%)

Computes Jaccard overlap on constitutional Articles, statutory Sections, and named Acts.

Jaccard(Ref_Q, Ref_Doc) = |Ref_Q ∩ Ref_Doc| / |Ref_Q ∪ Ref_Doc|
