KNOW YOUR RIGHTS

Judiciary Resources & Legal Awareness

Essential educational guidance regarding fundamental rights, legal procedures, and statutory protections in India.

01

Fundamental Rights

The Constitution protects equality, freedom of speech, religious freedom, cultural rights, and constitutional remedies.

  • Right to Equality (Articles 14–18)
  • Right to Freedom & Personal Liberty (Articles 19–22)
  • Right Against Exploitation (Articles 23–24)
  • Right to Constitutional Remedies (Article 32 & 226)
02

Rights of Women

Women are protected by specific statutory frameworks concerning safety, domestic peace, workplace dignity, and equal opportunity.

  • Protection Against Domestic Violence (DV Act 2005)
  • Workplace Harassment Protection (POSH Act)
  • Equal Remuneration & Property Rights
  • Right to Safe Legal Reporting Procedures
03

Consumer Rights

Consumers are legally entitled to remedies against defective products, deficiency of service, and unfair trade practices.

  • Right to Safety & Information
  • Right to Choose & Seek Redressal
  • District, State & National Consumer Commissions
  • Protection Against Misleading Advertisements
04

Arrest & Procedural Safeguards

The Code of Criminal Procedure and constitutional mandates protect individuals against unlawful arrest and custodial abuse.

  • Right to Know Grounds of Arrest (Section 50 CrPC)
  • Right to Consult Legal Practitioner
  • Production Before Magistrate Within 24 Hours
  • Mandatory DK Basu Guidelines Compliance
